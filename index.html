SPECIALIZED OVERSIGHT · BIOEQUIVALENCE & BIOAVAILABILITY

Independent quality oversight for BE/BA studies

Bioequivalence and bioavailability studies carry their own quality-critical checkpoints — from PK sampling windows to bioanalytical data traceability and statistical reporting. ClinGuard applies the same QA-led approach to this specialized study type.

Adherence to PK sampling time windows and washout periods
Bioanalytical data traceability from sample to reported result
Chain-of-custody review for biological sample handling
Verification of statistical analysis and 90% CI reporting for Cmax and AUC
Source data verification for subject compliance and protocol deviations

WHY QA-LED MONITORING MATTERS

An additional layer, not a replacement

ClinGuard complements operational CRA monitoring with a Quality Assurance mindset focused on risk, evidence and inspection readiness.

Traditional Operational Monitoring

  • Focused on day-to-day site and visit conduct
  • Tracks enrolment, visit windows and query resolution
  • Operates within the study's own reporting lines
  • Primarily forward-looking, visit to visit

ClinGuard QA-Led Oversight

  • Independent perspective on risk, compliance and evidence
  • Root-cause analysis and CAPA effectiveness verification
  • Structured toward inspection readiness
  • Trends findings across sites and visits for continuous improvement
